Our paper, Adversarial Rademacher Complexity of Deep Neural Networks, has been accepted for publication in JMLR. The paper has had a rather eventful journey. It was first submitted to ICLR 2022 and received scores of 8/8/8/6, but was not accepted. We subsequently submitted a revised version to JMLR, where the review process took longer than we had anticipated. We are very glad that the journey has now reached a positive conclusion. I am deeply grateful to everyone who supported and helped me along the way.
